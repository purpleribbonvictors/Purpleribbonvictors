Who we are

A voice, a purpose, a promise

Purple Ribbon Victors started with a simple belief. Epilepsy shouldn't define anyone, and no one living with it should have to face it alone. We work to close the gap between fear and understanding, one honest conversation at a time.

"Victors over seizures, united in hope."

Through community, real information, and awareness campaigns that actually change minds, we're building a space where people affected by seizures feel seen, feel supported, and never feel alone in it.

Our mission

What we're working toward, every day

We're here to help people living with seizure disorders feel supported, understood, and hopeful. That means real resources, real community, and awareness work that actually changes how people think and respond.

Our vision

The world we're building toward

A world where epilepsy doesn't come with silence or shame. Where every person affected by it is seen for who they are, not just their diagnosis, and where their community shows up for them without hesitation.

What we focus on

Three things we keep coming back to

Support

From first aid knowledge to everyday encouragement, we help people living with epilepsy, and the people who love them, feel less alone in it.

Awareness

We break down the myths that keep epilepsy wrapped in fear, and replace them with plain facts anyone can understand and pass on.

Community

Real stories, real connection. We're building a space where people can show up as themselves, diagnosis and all.

Something worth knowing

The real problem isn't epilepsy

It's misinformation. Fear creates assumptions, and assumptions become myths. Every time we replace a myth with the truth, we make our communities a little safer and a lot more compassionate.
